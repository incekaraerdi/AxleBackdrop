# AxleBackdrop TestFlight metadata (US English)

## Beta Description

AxleBackdrop turns five real car or motorcycle photos into a consistent, listing-ready set. Follow angle guides, apply one scene to both exterior views, compare every result with the original, and save or share five ordered JPEGs.

This beta processes photos on the iPhone. It does not include accounts, billing, marketplace integrations, or Digital Clean.

## What to Test

1. Create both a Car and Motorcycle listing.
2. Capture or import all five guided photos and confirm a draft survives relaunch.
3. Try Indoor Garage and Outdoor Scenic on both exterior angles.
4. Import an empty showroom as a dealer template, adjust both angles, save it, and reuse it in another listing.
5. Compare Original and Edited for every photo. Use Original if any vehicle detail or visible condition changes.
6. Save and share the five-photo set. Confirm photo 1 is clearly named as the cover.
7. Try System, Light, and Dark appearance and report any clipping, low contrast, slow processing, or failed cutout.

Please pay special attention to mirrors, handlebars, windscreens, wheel spokes, exhausts, decals, accessories, reflections, and gaps around motorcycle frames.

## External Beta App Review Notes

AxleBackdrop is a local-first automotive photo utility. No sign-in or purchase is required. Choose Car or Motorcycle, provide five photos, select a built-in scene, review the results, and export. Vehicle segmentation uses Apple Vision on device. The app never hides a failed edit: testers can select the original photo for any slot.

Camera access is used only to capture listing photos. Add-only Photos access is requested only when the user chooses Save 5 Photos. Imported source files and drafts remain in the app container until the draft is deleted.
