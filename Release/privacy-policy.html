Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Overview

AxleBackdrop ("App", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

What Data We Collect

Photos: The photos you take or import remain entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your photos on our servers.

Device Information: We do not collect device identifiers, phone numbers, or personal information.

Usage Data: We do not track analytics, crashes, or feature usage.

How We Use Your Data

All processing happens locally on your iPhone:

  • Photo compositing (vehicle extraction + background placement)
  • Image normalization for export
  • File storage in your device's local storage

No data leaves your device unless you explicitly export photos to your Photo Library or share them.

Third-Party Services

AxleBackdrop does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or cloud services in this version. All operations are device-local.

Your Photos

You Own Your Photos: All photos you capture or import are stored locally and remain your property.

Export to Photos: When you save to the Photos app, the images are added to your device's Photo Library with your permission.

Share: You can optionally share exported photos via standard iOS share sheets (Messages, Mail, etc.).

Camera & Photo Library Permissions

The app requests permission to:

  • Camera: To capture vehicle photos directly
  • Photo Library: To import existing photos and export results

You can revoke these permissions in Settings → AxleBackdrop anytime.

Data Retention

All draft listings and processed photos are stored on your device until you delete them. Deleting a draft removes all associated photos and processed files.

Children's Privacy

AxleBackdrop is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Photos are protected by your device's built-in security (Secure Enclave, file permissions). The app does not use encryption beyond what iOS provides by default.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Your continued use constitutes acceptance.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy?

Response Time: We aim to respond within 48 hours.
